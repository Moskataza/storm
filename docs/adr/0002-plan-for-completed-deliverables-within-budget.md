# Plan for completed deliverables within the task budget

Plan the whole task's scope, source acquisition, processing, and schedule against its time and cost allowances before execution, reserving effort for analysis, writing, visuals, export, and result checking. Adapt lower-priority research to preserve delivery of the requested report, rather than making a budget-triggered pause the default behavior: the operator needs an achievable work plan and a completed deliverable with explicit coverage limits.

This favors a budget-shaped scope over a fixed research depth for every request. Estimates should be informed by measured usage and timings, with unavailable evidence and remaining uncertainty visible in the delivered report.
